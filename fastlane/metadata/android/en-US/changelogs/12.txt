First release on F-Droid.

Send mail from any address on a domain you own: you pick the part before the @ and the display name every time you write.

• Free-form sender identity, per-domain SMTP with STARTTLS or implicit TLS
• Rich text, attachments, inline images, custom headers
• Drafts, a retrying outbox and a sent log
• Passwords kept in the Android Keystore
• No inbox, no account, no tracking; SQLite built from source
